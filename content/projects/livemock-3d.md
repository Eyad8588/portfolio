## Overview

LiveMock 3D is a desktop app (Electron + Three.js, strict TypeScript) that puts any screen content — a live URL, images or video — onto a 3D device such as an iPhone or a MacBook, animates it with a keyframe timeline and camera moves, and exports a ready-to-edit video. Free and open source (MIT), with no accounts, watermarks or telemetry. Runs on macOS and Windows.

## Features

- Live web pages rendered on the device screen, including right-to-left (Arabic) layouts.
- Custom GLB device models with automatic screen mapping and real-world scale.
- Environments (studio gradient, reflective floor, dark cinematic, transparent…) and post effects (bloom, vignette, film grain, depth of field).
- Timeline with keyframes, easing, scenes and camera moves; video export.
