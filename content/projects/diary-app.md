## Overview

A mobile diary app for iOS and Android built with React Native and Expo. Instead of typing, you record your day as audio or video, and your stories are stored online in Firebase.

## Features

- Login screen.
- A "ringing" reminder screen with a ringing sound that prompts you to record.
- Choose between viewing past stories, recording audio or recording video.
- Audio recording saved to Firebase Storage.
- Video recording (expo-camera) saved to Firebase Storage.
- Past stories listed from their Firebase links.

## Stack

React Native, Expo (expo-av, expo-camera, expo-media-library), React Navigation, Firebase.
